# Fotografía provisional

Se generaron cinco imágenes con la herramienta integrada ImageGen para la dirección Soft Editorial Boutique. No son fotografías de productos reales de Corinne. La interfaz las identifica como ilustrativas. Los originales permanecen en la carpeta de imágenes generadas de Codex; las copias del proyecto se convirtieron a WebP con Sharp, sin cambios en el contenido.

Archivos finales, relativos al proyecto:

- `src/assets/photography/hero-cake.webp`
- `src/assets/photography/brownies.webp`
- `src/assets/photography/cubiletes.webp`
- `src/assets/photography/artisan-hands.webp`
- `src/assets/photography/seasonal-cake.webp`

El logotipo es exclusivamente el SVG oficial provisto por el usuario; no fue generado ni rediseñado.

## Prompts usados

### hero-cake

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bakery boutique website hero, tall portrait 4:5 crop. Primary request: an exceptionally natural editorial food photograph of an artisan round ivory vanilla buttercream layer cake with subtle piped scallops, six fresh raspberries with tiny leaves arranged on top, and a few chamomile flowers. Cake on a white ceramic pedestal on an ivory linen-covered table, small white flowers in a clear glass vase out of focus at the far edge, a silver vintage dessert fork on crumpled linen. Airy daylight entering from the left. The cake occupies the lower two thirds of the portrait photograph with breathing room above. Camera slightly above table eye level, 70mm lens, gentle depth of field, fine real texture, contemporary European pastry magazine feel, natural imperfect buttercream, white and soft grey backdrop with subtle sage foliage. No text, labels, logos, borders, watermark, graphic design, collage or illustrations. This is provisional concept photography, not a real bakery product.

### brownies

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: boutique bakery product portrait 3:4. Primary request: a beautiful realistic food photograph of three thick dark chocolate brownies stacked slightly off-center on parchment and a small white ceramic plate. Crackled shiny tops, fudgy interior, a few natural crumbs. White linen tabletop, soft cool-white wall in background, side daylight, subtle shadow, editorial closeup photographed with 85mm lens. Dark chocolate against luminous whites. Relaxed, real handmade patisserie mood. No text, logos, hands, watermark, graphic design or collage. Provisional product concept photography.

### cubiletes

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: boutique bakery product portrait 3:4. Primary request: natural editorial food photography of three Mexican cubiletes de queso, little individual golden brown pastry cups filled with soft cheesecake, one cut in half showing the pale filling. Small ivory ceramic plate on white linen, tiny crumbs, bright natural daylight with gentle shadows, very light warm grey studio wall. Photo with 85mm lens, close-up composition with ample breathing room. Contemporary artisanal pastry magazine, tactile pastry texture and crimped pastry edges. No text, logos, watermark, graphic design or collage. Provisional product concept photography.

### artisan-hands

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: boutique bakery story section, landscape 4:3. Primary request: a candid closeup photograph showing only a pastry baker's two hands using a metal offset spatula to carefully frost a small ivory buttercream cake on a metal cake turntable. White apron torso, head out of frame. Quiet white bakery worktable with a ceramic bowl of frosting, stainless whisk, loosely folded linen cloth and some flour. Side window natural daylight, rich tactile texture, film-like but clear, intimate craft editorial. Calm neutral whites with a few green leaves out of focus. Anatomically correct hands. No text, branding, logo, border, watermark or collage. Provisional editorial concept photography.

### seasonal-cake

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: bakery seasonal campaign, landscape 3:2. Primary request: sophisticated natural editorial food photograph of a small handmade autumn spiced pear cake on a pale ceramic pedestal. Ivory cream, poached pear slices and delicate cinnamon dust decorating its top, a cut slice on a small white plate in the foreground. Whole pear on linen, a simple green leaf, white linen table, pale grey-white wall. Bright soft window daylight, restrained contemporary still life, generous negative space to the left and main cake right of center. No pumpkins, no orange decorative background. Real textures, 70mm lens. No text, logo, label, border, watermark or collage. Provisional seasonal product concept photography.
